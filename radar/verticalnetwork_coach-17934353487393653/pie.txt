SWINGING OUT on an edge? 👀

The problem is often not your strength — it’s your position.

Keep your arms long, place your feet around the edge and create pressure through your feet.

Then build the movement step by step:
Feet up → set the hook → hands up → repeat.

Less pulling. More tension. More control. 🧗
