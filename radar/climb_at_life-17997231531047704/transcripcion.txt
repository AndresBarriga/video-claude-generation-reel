[  0.0–  3.7] You don't need a bigger jump. You need a bigger backswing.
[  3.7– 10.3] Three rules to stick it. Rule three is how Colin Duffy stuck the Olympic swing nobody else could.
[ 10.3– 16.9] Rule one, aim your hips. Before you swing, turn your hips to face the hold. Your body flies where
[ 16.9– 23.8] your hips point. Rule two, swing back big. Knees up, kick out, arms straight. Let your
[ 23.8– 29.0] legs swing back high. The higher they swing back, the more speed you have coming forward.
[ 29.0– 34.2] Rule three, stop the swing. At the bottom, pull and kick your legs through.
[ 34.2– 40.6] Eyes on the landing. Feet touch first, then push your hips in. The swing dies and you stay on.
[ 40.6– 46.3] That's how Duffy stuck it in Paris. Save this. Climber will teach this in a lesson and track
