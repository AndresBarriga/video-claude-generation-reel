Climbing isn’t hard. Carl is pulling. 🧗

Most beginners climb on their arms. Bent elbows, hips away from the wall, feet just along for the ride. Your forearms are toast in 30 seconds.

Try this on your next move:
1. Feet first – place your foot before you reach
2. Set the hips – get them over that foot, arms long
3. Then move – let your legs stand you up

Position first. Move second.

Save this and try it on your next session. Are you a Carl? ↓
