Stop hanging from roofs 🦇 A study in Grenoble put 7 climbers on force-sensing holds. On vertical walls their feet carried 57% of the load. On overhangs, 62% went into their hands.

The fix is toes, belly, hips:
1️⃣ Push your toes into the holds
2️⃣ Squeeze your belly and keep your body tight
3️⃣ Pull your hips up toward the roof

That’s how a 5'2" climber stays on in Innsbruck. A Climbr breakdown, app coming soon 🐐 Send this to the friend who hangs on every roof.
