The Inside Flag isn’t poor technique, it’s just misundestood... 

Sometimes called a “through flag,” “Can-Can” or “Ferret Launcher” (lie), this technique can be used in a few situations on the wall to resist rotation and achieve stability during reaches.

If your backflag is limited by your other leg(s), and/or the position requires more rotational opposition against the hold you are reaching from, the inside flag can often be superior. 

The inside flag can, however, lead to some pretty nasty dead-ends, in which unwinding from them can be absolutely catastrophic and result in a fall, off of and away from the rock climbing wall upon which you were participating upon.
