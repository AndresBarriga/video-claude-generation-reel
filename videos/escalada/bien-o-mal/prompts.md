# Cuerda detrás de la pierna: imágenes IA (prueba 23-nov)
Herramienta: Gemini (Nano Banana 2, gratis). Clave: generar la ✕ y luego EDITAR esa misma imagen cambiando solo la cuerda → ✓ idéntica salvo la cuerda.
Referencia de composición: subir la imagen de @sima.k202 del caso y pedir «misma situación de cuerda y pierna, pero con este personaje, este estilo y otro ángulo/roca». Que quede distinto (personaje, colores, encuadre).
Pedir siempre 9:16 y sin texto. Si sale marca de agua, se recorta/tapa en el montaje.

Estilo (pegar al principio de cada prompt):
Semi-realistic digital painting with clean comic ink outlines, cel shading, warm natural afternoon light, highly detailed grey granite with lichen and cracks, vertical 9:16, no text, no logos, no watermark.

Personaje (mismo en todas):
A female sport climber: dark curly hair in a bun, striped mustard-and-teal t-shirt, grey climbing pants rolled at the ankle, blue climbing shoes, red harness, bright red dynamic rope.

1. Personaje: [estilo] [personaje] lead climbing a granite slab, seen from the side and slightly below, a quickdraw with the red rope clipped just below her feet.
2. ✕ primer plano: [estilo] Close-up from the side at foot level: her right foot on a small foothold, the bright red rope coming down from her harness wraps around the OUTSIDE of her right calf, so her leg is trapped between the rope and the rock, then goes to the quickdraw below. The rope must be clearly visible crossing over the calf.
3. ✓ (editar la 2): Same image, same pose, same light. Change only the rope: it now runs straight from the harness to the quickdraw between her leg and the rock, never touching or crossing the leg.
4. Consecuencia: [estilo] [personaje] falling backwards, the red rope caught behind her calf flips her upside down, head toward the rock, motion lines, no injury shown.
5. Truco: [estilo] [personaje] mid-climb looking down at her own legs and rope before stepping up, focused face.
6. Compañero: [estilo] A belayer on the ground with a grigri, pointing up and shouting, seen from behind and below, the climber small above.
Encima, en Remotion: zoom lento, la cuerda resaltada con un trazo que la recorre, ✕/✓ animados, flecha del tirón y el volteo.
